Background & Setting

The Deathwatch — Chambers of Vigilance
Ordo Xenos — Long Vigil Standing Orders

The Deathwatch are the elite xenos-hunting arm of the Inquisition's Ordo Xenos — a brotherhood unlike any other in the Imperium. They are not a Chapter. They are a calling. Warriors drawn from across hundreds of Space Marine Chapters, each bearing the ancient rites of their birth-brotherhood yet bound by a higher oath: the Long Vigil against the alien.

When a Space Marine swears himself to the Deathwatch, he sets aside the colours of his Chapter and dons the black armour of the Vigil. Only his left shoulder pad remains, bearing the heraldry of his home Chapter — a reminder of what he was, and a symbol of the brotherhood that watches over him still. He does not know when his Vigil will end. He serves until the Watch Commander releases him, or until he falls in service to the Throne.

They do not fight in armies. They fight in Kill Teams — small, precise, and utterly lethal — deployed against threats that require surgical elimination rather than brute force. A single Kill Team of Deathwatch has broken xenos invasions that entire Imperial Guard armies could not contain.

The Jericho Reach — Sector Overview
Segmentum Ultima  ·  Classification: Volatile

The Jericho Reach is a vast stretch of space in Segmentum Ultima, connected to the wider Imperium by a single stable Warp corridor known as the Jericho-Maw Warp Gate. Once a glittering network of prosperous Imperial worlds, the Reach was sundered by the Age of Shadow — millennia of warp storms and isolation that reduced its colonies to silence.

Now the Imperium pushes back into the Reach through the Achilus Crusade, seeking to reclaim what was lost. But the Reach does not give up its secrets easily. Tyranid splinter fleets haunt the space lanes. Heretic warbands have been sighted in the outer systems, and disturbing reports from excavation teams speak of ancient machine-things stirring beneath the surfaces of long-dead worlds. And amid the returning light of the Imperium, something else grows — quiet, patient, deeply rooted in the flesh of those it would claim.

Kill Team Assembled at Watch Fortress Erioch

Deathwatch

Watch Commander Mordigael has called the Kill Team to the fortress's Strategium. Each Battle-Brother has been drawn from their Chapter by the black ships of the Deathwatch and brought to Erioch to swear the Long Vigil. Their first deployment together will be Mataras II. They are strangers to one another. They will not remain so.

Chaplain

Deathwatch Specialty  ·  D&D 5e Homebrew Ruleset  ·  Jericho Reach Campaign

The Chaplain is the spiritual heart of any Space Marine formation — the keeper of oaths, the guardian of honour, and the voice of the Emperor's wrath given ceramite and sinew. Where others doubt in the darkest hour, the Chaplain does not. Their faith is not a comfort but a weapon, a beacon that drives warriors past the limits of flesh and will, and a hammer that breaks the morale of enemies who were beginning to believe they might survive the engagement.

Armed with the Crozius Arcanum — equal parts weapon, relic, and symbol of office — and able to call upon the Emperor's Blessing to invoke Holy Powers that shake the ground with divine fury, the Chaplain is as formidable in close combat as they are essential to the spiritual cohesion of the kill team. Their Litanies ring across any battlefield, turning doubt to resolve and resolve to devastation.

Emperor's Blessing Points & Litany Charges
Emperor's Blessing Points (EBP) are spent to invoke Holy Powers (The Chaplain & Holy Powers sheet). Base Holy Spells are free, unlimited, and never need to be prepared — they function like cantrips. Save DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + CHA modifier. EBP resets on resupply.
Prayer Recovery: Once per resupply, during a short rest you may spend time in focused devotion and recover EBP equal to your CHA modifier × Proficiency Bonus. This represents drawing fresh strength from your faith rather than exhausting your reserves — it requires no roll and carries no cost.
Exalt: When invoking any Holy Power, you may spend additional EBP — up to your CHA modifier — to Exalt it. Damage powers deal +1d6 radiant damage per additional EBP spent. Support or control powers instead extend their duration by 50% or affect one additional valid target per additional EBP (declare before invoking). The extra EBP is spent regardless of whether the power succeeds.
Litany Charges equal Proficiency Bonus per resupply (increases to Prof Bonus + CHA modifier at level 17). Litanies are spoken as a bonus action at any range. You may not use a Litany and a Crozius Rite on the same turn — both require your bonus action.

Class Features
Level 1Word of the Emperor

Your voice carries the weight of the Emperor's will. You gain proficiency with the Crozius Arcanum and its Faithful Strike modifier. You learn the art of the Litany — powerful invocations spoken as a bonus action that function at any range. You begin each resupply with Litany Charges equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You may not use a Litany and a Crozius Rite on the same turn — both require your bonus action.

You begin knowing three Litanies and learn one more at levels 5, 9, 13, and 17. See the Litanies section below.

Level 1Emperor's Blessing

You may spend Emperor's Blessing Points to invoke Holy Powers from The Chaplain & Holy Powers sheet, provided you meet the Devotion requirement for that power. Base Holy Spells are free, unlimited, and never need to be prepared — they function like cantrips and are always available. The save DC for all Holy Powers is 8 + Proficiency Bonus + CHA modifier. Emperor's Blessing Points reset on resupply.

Preparation. Before each mission, choose Holy Powers to prepare equal to your Chaplain Level + CHA modifier. You may only invoke prepared Holy Powers. Prepared powers can only be changed on resupply. Base Holy Spells are always available and never count against this limit.

Faith's Price. As a bonus action, you may accept physical suffering as a testament to your unshakeable faith. Spend up to 10 HP — gain 1 EBP per 2 HP spent (round down, minimum 1 HP spent). This HP loss cannot be prevented or restored by any means until after your next resupply. Every use beyond the first per resupply requires a DC 12 WIS save — on a failure, suffer 1 level of Exhaustion as the flesh strains beyond what faith alone can sustain.

Level 2Crozius Rites

While wielding your Crozius Arcanum you may project the Emperor's judgment as a bonus action, choosing one target you can see within 30ft. You may not use a Crozius Rite and a Litany on the same turn. Choose one Rite:

  • Rite of Censure: The target must make a WIS saving throw (DC = 8 + Prof Bonus + CHA modifier) or be Frightened of you until the end of their next turn.
  • Rite of Purgation: The target takes 2d6 radiant damage and cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn.
  • Rite of Absolution: One ally within 30ft immediately removes one condition affecting them as the Emperor's light cleanses them.

You may use Crozius Rites a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per resupply.

Level 3Aura of Zeal

Your unwavering faith radiates outward as a permanent 10ft aura. Allies within the aura gain +1 to attack rolls and cannot be surprised while you are conscious. You personally gain +2 to attack rolls and advantage on saving throws against being Frightened or Charmed while your aura is active. The aura improves at levels 9 (15ft, allies +2, you +3) and 14 (20ft).

Level 5Extra Attack

When you take the Attack action you can make 2 attacks instead of 1.

Crozius Resonance. Your Crozius strikes and your holy invocations reinforce one another. When you hit with your Crozius Arcanum, your next Holy Power manifested before the end of your next turn costs 2 fewer EBP (minimum 1). This reduction applies once per turn regardless of how many hits land.

Level 6Empowered Litanies

When you use a Litany you may choose to empower it by spending 2 Emperor's Blessing Points to double its effect for that use: Litany of Hate deals +2d4 damage; Litany of Faith grants advantage on two saving throws; Litany of Endurance doubles temporary HP granted; Litany of Wrath applies maximum damage to the next two hits instead of one.

Level 11Divine Wrath

Once per turn as a bonus action while wielding your Crozius Arcanum, you may spend 3 Emperor's Blessing Points to project a burst of holy energy. All enemies within 15ft of you must make a CON saving throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + CHA modifier) or take 2d8 radiant damage.

Level 13Oathbound

You become immune to both the Frightened and Charmed conditions — no force in the galaxy can shake the faith of the Emperor's spiritual guardian. Allies within your Aura of Zeal cannot be Charmed and have advantage on saving throws against Corruption effects.

Level 14Aura of Redemption

Your Aura of Zeal expands to 20ft. Additionally, once per resupply when an ally within your aura would drop to 0 HP, you may use your reaction to spend 5 Emperor's Blessing Points — the damage is reduced to 0 and the ally remains at 1 HP, bolstered by the Emperor's grace.

Level 17Voice of Dominion

Your Litany Charges increase to Proficiency Bonus + CHA modifier per resupply. Crozius Rites no longer consume uses — they are free to activate every time, limited only by the bonus action economy.

Level 18Vessel of the Emperor

Your faith has transformed you into a living conduit of the Emperor's will. You gain resistance to psychic and radiant damage. Your CHA score increases by 2 and its maximum increases to 22. All Holy Powers cast through Emperor's Blessing cost 2 fewer points (minimum 1).

Level 20Eternal Crusade — Class Paragon

Once per resupply as an action, for 1 minute you become the Emperor's voice made manifest. All Litanies require no Litany Charges for the duration. Your Aura of Zeal expands to 30ft — allies gain +4 to attack rolls and you gain +5. All allies within the aura gain immunity to Frightened and Charmed for the duration. Your Crozius Arcanum deals an additional 2d8 radiant damage on every hit. Enemies that start their turn within 15ft of you must make a WIS saving throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + CHA modifier) or be Frightened until the end of their turn.

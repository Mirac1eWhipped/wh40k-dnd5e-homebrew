## Gargoyle

*Small Beast (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 16 (3d6 + 6)  
**Speed** 10 ft., fly 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 2 (−4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) |

---

**Skills** Perception +2  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Synapse Dependency.*** If no Synapse creature is within 30 feet, the Gargoyle must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or use its action to attack the nearest creature, regardless of allegiance.

***Flyby.*** The Gargoyle doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

***Swooping Descent.*** If the Gargoyle dives at least 20 feet straight down toward a target and hits with a Talons attack on the same turn, the target must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Actions

***Fleshborer.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or take 2 (1d4) poison damage at the start of each of its turns for 1 minute (save ends).

***Talons.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) slashing damage.

***Blinding Venom (Recharge 5–6).*** The Gargoyle spits a cone of corrosive bio-fluid. Each creature in a 15-foot cone must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn. On a success, the creature is not blinded.

## Ravener

*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 60 (8d10 + 16)  
**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 4 (−3) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +6, Dex +5  
**Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +5  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Synapse Dependency.*** If no Synapse creature is within 30 feet, the Ravener must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or use its action to attack the nearest creature, regardless of allegiance.

***Subterranean Assault.*** When the Ravener erupts from underground (having burrowed at least 10 feet on the same turn), each creature within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or take 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone from the eruption.

***Tunnel.*** The Ravener leaves a 5-foot-wide, 5-foot-tall tunnel in its wake when it burrows. Other creatures can move through this tunnel normally.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Ravener makes two attacks: one with its Rending Claws and one with its Bite or Spinefists.

***Rending Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is prone, this attack deals an extra 7 (2d6) piercing damage as the Ravener bears down on it.

***Spinefists.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### Bonus Actions

***Burrow.*** The Ravener moves up to its burrow speed, either moving underground or erupting to the surface.

## Lictor

*Medium Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 6 (−2) | 14 (+2) | 6 (−2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Str +5  
**Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +9  
**Condition Immunities** frightened  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 16  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

> *The Lictor has expertise in Perception and Stealth (double proficiency bonus already included above).*

---

### Traits

***Chameleonic Skin.*** The Lictor has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. While it remains motionless, it is invisible until it attacks or takes damage.

***Spider Climb.*** The Lictor can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Pheromone Trail.*** When the Lictor kills a creature, it releases hunter-pheromones. Any Tyranid creature that moves within 30 feet of the kill site within the next hour gains advantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.

***Lone Hunter.*** The Lictor has advantage on initiative rolls and can't be surprised while it is conscious.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Lictor makes three attacks: two with its Flesh Hooks and one with its Scything Talons.

***Flesh Hooks.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) piercing damage. If both Flesh Hook attacks hit the same target on the same turn, the target is grappled (escape DC 13) and restrained until the grapple ends.

***Scything Talons.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage. If the target is grappled by the Lictor, this attack deals an extra 7 (2d6) slashing damage as the talons find no resistance.

### Bonus Actions

***Vanish.*** If the Lictor has not attacked this turn, it becomes invisible until the start of its next turn, until it attacks, or until it takes damage.

## Neurothrope

*Medium Aberration (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 17 (psychic aegis)  
**Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)  
**Speed** 20 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 8 (−1) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 19 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 8 (−1) |

---

**Saving Throws** Int +7, Wis +6  
**Skills** Arcana +7, Perception +6  
**Damage Resistances** psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks (while psychic aegis is active)  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened  
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., truesight 60 ft., passive Perception 16  
**Languages** — (telepathy 120 ft.)  
**Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

### Traits

***Synapse Creature (Enhanced).*** The Neurothrope projects a synapse field within 60 feet. Tyranid creatures with the Synapse Dependency trait in range automatically pass those saves, gain advantage on saving throws against being frightened and charmed, and add +2 to their attack rolls.

***Psychic Aegis (Reinforced).*** The Neurothrope's warp field provides its AC of 17 and resistance to nonmagical physical damage. The field does not collapse on incapacitation — instead, it collapses only if the Neurothrope takes 20 or more damage from a single attack, and reforms at the start of its next turn.

***Warp Siphon.*** Whenever the Neurothrope deals psychic damage, it regains 7 (2d6) hit points.

***Hive Mind Node.*** Tyranid creatures with Synapse Dependency within 60 feet of the Neurothrope may use the Neurothrope's Intelligence modifier (+4) in place of their own Wisdom modifier when making Synapse Dependency saving throws.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Neurothrope uses two Psychic Powers (any combination below).

***Warp Blast.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 150 ft., one target. *Hit:* 24 (7d6) psychic damage.

***Psychic Shriek (Recharge 5–6).*** Each creature within 40 feet must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw or take 35 (10d6) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of the Neurothrope's next turn. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not stunned.

***Synaptic Backlash (Recharge 6).*** One creature the Neurothrope can see within 60 feet must make a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes 42 (12d6) psychic damage and has its Intelligence score reduced by 1d6 (minimum 1). A creature whose Intelligence is reduced to 3 or lower is incapacitated. The reduction recovers after a long rest. On a success, the creature takes half damage with no other effects.

***Ectoplasmic Manifestation.*** The Neurothrope twists the warp into a 15-foot cube centered on a point within 60 feet. The area becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute. Any creature that starts its turn in the area takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage.

### Reactions

***Aetheric Shield.*** When the Neurothrope or a Tyranid creature within 30 feet of it is targeted by an attack, the Neurothrope can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll.

## Trygon

*Huge Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 178 (17d12 + 68)  
**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 26 (+8) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 2 (−4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +12, Con +8  
**Skills** Athletics +12  
**Damage Immunities** lightning  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +4

---

### Traits

***Siege Monster.*** The Trygon deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Subterranean Assault.*** When the Trygon erupts from underground (having burrowed at least 20 feet on the same turn), each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or take 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not knocked prone.

***Tunnel.*** The Trygon leaves a 10-foot-wide, 10-foot-tall tunnel in its wake when it burrows, large enough for Medium and smaller creatures to move through freely.

***Bio-Electric Field.*** Any creature that grapples the Trygon or that the Trygon is grappling takes 5 (1d10) lightning damage at the start of each of the Trygon's turns.

***Death from Below.*** If the Trygon burrows at least 20 feet on its turn before erupting, the first attack it makes on that turn has advantage and deals an extra 10 (3d6) bludgeoning damage.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Trygon makes three attacks: two with its Rending Claws and one with its Tail Sweep. It can replace the Tail Sweep with a Bio-Electric Discharge if available.

***Rending Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (3d8 + 8) slashing damage. If both Rending Claw attacks hit the same target on the same turn, the target is grappled (escape DC 20) and restrained.

***Tail Sweep.*** Each creature within 10 feet directly behind the Trygon must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or take 19 (2d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not knocked prone.

***Bio-Electric Discharge (Recharge 5–6).*** The Trygon releases a surge of bio-electric energy in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or take 45 (10d8) lightning damage, or half as much on a success. Creatures wearing metal armor that fail the save take an additional 11 (2d10) lightning damage as the current amplifies through their gear.

### Bonus Actions

***Burrow.*** The Trygon moves up to its burrow speed. If it begins its movement on the surface, it descends underground; if it begins underground, it erupts to the surface at any point along its burrowed path.

## Hive Tyrant

*Huge Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 19 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 230 (20d12 + 100)  
**Speed** 40 ft. *(or 40 ft., fly 30 ft. with Wings variant)*

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 25 (+7) | 14 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +12, Con +10, Int +9, Wis +8  
**Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +8, Intimidation +6  
**Damage Resistances** psychic, poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., truesight 60 ft., passive Perception 18  
**Languages** — (telepathy 120 ft.)  
**Challenge** 14 (11,500 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +5

---

### Traits

***Supreme Synapse Creature.*** The Hive Tyrant projects a synapse field in a 60-foot radius. All Tyranid creatures in range automatically pass Synapse Dependency saves, gain advantage on saving throws against being charmed and frightened, and add the Hive Tyrant's proficiency bonus (+5) to their damage rolls.

***Magic Resistance.*** The Hive Tyrant has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the Hive Tyrant fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Relentless (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, the Hive Tyrant drops to 1 hit point instead.

***Warlord of the Hive.*** When the Hive Tyrant takes the Attack action, all Tyranid creatures it can see within 30 feet can use their reaction to make one weapon attack.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Hive Tyrant makes four attacks: two with its Monstrous Rending Claws and two with its Bonesword Lashes. It can replace both Bonesword Lashes with one use of its Bio-cannon.

***Monstrous Rending Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) slashing damage. If both Rending Claw attacks hit the same target in one turn, that target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or be incapacitated until the end of its next turn from the trauma of the wounds.

***Bonesword Lash.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (3d6 + 7) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (save ends at end of each of its turns).

***Bio-cannon.*** Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point the Hive Tyrant can see within 120 feet must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. Creatures that fail are coated in bio-acid, taking 4 (1d8) acid damage at the start of each of their turns until they use an action to remove it.

***Psych-Scream (Recharge 5–6).*** The Hive Tyrant projects a psychic roar. Each creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of the Hive Tyrant's next turn. While frightened this way, a creature must use its action to Dash away from the Hive Tyrant on each of its turns.

### Legendary Actions

The Hive Tyrant can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Spent legendary actions are regained at the start of the Hive Tyrant's turn.

***Strike.*** The Hive Tyrant makes one Monstrous Rending Claws attack.

***Warp Pulse (Costs 2 Actions).*** A burst of psychic force erupts in a 20-foot radius around the Hive Tyrant. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw or take 21 (6d6) psychic damage and be pushed 15 feet away. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not pushed.

***Command the Swarm (Costs 2 Actions).*** All Tyranid creatures the Hive Tyrant can see can immediately move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Feeding Frenzy (Costs 3 Actions).*** The Hive Tyrant makes a full Multiattack.

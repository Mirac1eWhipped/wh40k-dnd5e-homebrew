✠ How Holy Powers Work — Chaplain Reference

All Holy Powers draw upon Emperor's Blessing Points (EBP) — the measure of your faith's reach into the divine. Your EBP pool resets fully on resupply. Your Save DC for all Holy Powers is 8 + Proficiency Bonus + CHA modifier.

Powers come in two categories. Base Holy Spells are always available, always free, and never need to be prepared — use them without limit like cantrips. Holy Powers cost EBP and come in three Devotion tiers: Devotion I costs 4–5 EBP and is available from Level 1. Devotion II costs 8–12 EBP and requires Level 7+. Devotion III costs 12–16 EBP and requires Level 13+. Before each mission, prepare Holy Powers equal to your Chaplain Level + CHA modifier. Prepared powers can only change on resupply.

Exalt: When invoking any Holy Power, spend up to your CHA modifier in additional EBP to Exalt it. Damage powers gain +1d6 radiant per extra EBP. Support/control powers extend duration by 50% or gain one extra target per extra EBP (declare before invoking). Crozius Resonance (Level 5+): hitting with your Crozius before invoking reduces the next Holy Power's cost by 2 EBP (minimum 1, once per turn). Prayer Recovery: once per resupply, recover CHA mod × Prof Bonus EBP during a short rest.

Litany Charges are separate from EBP and power Litanies (spoken as bonus actions from your class sheet). You may not use a Litany and a Crozius Rite on the same turn. Empowered Litanies (Level 6+) cost 2 EBP per use.

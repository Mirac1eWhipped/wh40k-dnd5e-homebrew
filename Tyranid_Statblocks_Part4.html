Traits

Pack Tactics. The leaper has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
Leaping Pounce. If the leaper moves at least 15 feet toward a creature and hits it with a Rending Claws attack on the same turn, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If knocked prone, the leaper may immediately make one Bite attack as a bonus action.
Synapse Dependency. While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, the leaper loses Pack Tactics and acts erratically, making a DC 10 Wisdom save at the start of each turn or attacking the nearest creature.

Actions

Multiattack. The leaper makes two Rending Claws attacks.
Rending Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d4 + 4) slashing damage.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, plus 4 (1d8) additional piercing damage if the target is prone.

Bonus Actions

Leap. The leaper jumps up to 30 feet horizontally or 15 feet vertically without provoking opportunity attacks. This does not count against its speed.

Traits

Psychic Interference. Any creature that attempts to cast a spell or use a psionic ability within 30 feet of the Neurolictor must first succeed on a DC 14 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the spell or ability fails without expending the slot or use.
Shadow in the Warp. The Neurolictor is invisible in areas of dim light or darkness. It can still be detected by truesight or by hearing (Perception with disadvantage).
Spider Climb. The Neurolictor can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without ability checks.
Synapse Dependency. While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, the Neurolictor loses Psychic Interference and deals 1d6 less damage on its attacks.

Actions

Multiattack. The Neurolictor makes two attacks: one Neurolash and one Flesh Hooks.
Neurolash. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
Flesh Hooks. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d4 + 5) piercing damage. If Medium or smaller, the target is grappled (escape DC 15) and pulled up to 5 feet toward the Neurolictor.

Reactions

Psychic Deflection. When targeted by a spell that requires an attack roll, the Neurolictor uses its reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll.

Skills Perception +9 (Expertise), Stealth +13 (Expertise), Athletics +7

Languages —

Proficiency Bonus +3

Traits

Shadow in the Warp. Death Leaper is invisible in areas of dim light or darkness. It can still be detected by truesight.
Spider Climb. Death Leaper can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without ability checks.
Phantom Menace. When Death Leaper kills a creature while invisible, each creature that witnessed the kill within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom save or be frightened until the end of its next turn.
Assassin's Focus. Death Leaper has advantage on attack rolls against creatures that haven't taken a turn yet in combat, or against creatures it has grappled.
Synapse Dependency. While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, Death Leaper loses Shadow in the Warp and deals 1d8 less damage on its attacks.

Actions

Multiattack. Death Leaper makes two attacks: one Flesh Hooks and one Scything Talons, or two Scything Talons.
Flesh Hooks. Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d6 + 7) piercing damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 15) and restrained until the grapple ends.
Scything Talons. Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (4d6 + 7) slashing damage, plus an extra 10 (3d6) slashing damage if the target is grappled by Death Leaper.

Bonus Actions

Vanish. Death Leaper moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks and becomes invisible until the start of its next turn or until it attacks.

Legendary Actions

Death Leaper can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Legendary actions are regained at the start of Death Leaper's turn.

Skitter. Death Leaper moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
Lashing Strike (Costs 2 Actions). Death Leaper makes one Flesh Hooks attack. If it hits and the target is already grappled, it automatically deals the bonus damage from Scything Talons.

Condition Immunities Frightened, Prone

Senses Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16

Languages —

Proficiency Bonus +5

Traits

Living Carrier. The Harridan can carry up to 3 Small or smaller Tyranid creatures on its underbelly. It can release one carried creature as a bonus action, depositing it in any unoccupied space within 10 feet.
Sweeping Wings. When the Harridan moves on the ground, each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Strength save or be knocked prone.
Siege Monster. The Harridan deals double damage to objects and structures.
Synapse Node. The Harridan counts as a Synapse creature. Friendly Tyranid creatures within 60 feet are immune to Synapse Dependency.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the Harridan fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

Actions

Multiattack. The Harridan makes three attacks: two Crushing Claws and one Bio-Salvo, or three Crushing Claws attacks.
Crushing Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage. Large or smaller creatures must succeed on a DC 18 Strength save or be grappled (escape DC 18).
Bio-Salvo. The Harridan spews corrosive ichor in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area makes a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half on a success.
Wing Buffet (Recharge 5–6). Each creature within 20 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Strength save or take 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage and be thrown up to 20 feet and knocked prone. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is neither thrown nor knocked prone.

Legendary Actions

The Harridan can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Legendary actions are regained at the start of the Harridan's turn.

Sweep. The Harridan makes one Crushing Claw attack.
Reposition. The Harridan moves up to half its fly speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
Release Brood (Costs 2 Actions). The Harridan releases all carried Tyranid creatures into unoccupied spaces within 15 feet. Those creatures may immediately take their full turn.

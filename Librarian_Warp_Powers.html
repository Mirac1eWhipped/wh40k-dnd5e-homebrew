◇ How Warp Powers Work — Librarian Reference

All Librarian powers draw on Warp Resilience Points (WRP) — the measure of how hard you are pushing into the Immaterium. Your WRP pool resets fully on resupply, and you may recover INT mod × Prof Bonus WRP once per resupply on a short rest by pulling back from the Warp (no risk, no roll). Your Save DC for all powers is 8 + Proficiency Bonus + INT modifier.

Powers come in two categories. Base Powers are always available, always free, and never carry any risk — use them freely and without limit. Advanced Powers cost WRP and come in four ranks: Rank I costs 4–6 WRP, Rank II costs 8–12 WRP, Rank III costs 12–18 WRP, and Rank IV costs 18–24 WRP. Higher ranks unlock at higher Librarian levels. Before each mission, you prepare a number of Advanced Powers equal to your Librarian Level + INT modifier — this can only change on resupply. Base Powers and your discipline's 4 auto-prepared powers never count against this limit.

Whenever you spend 6 or more WRP on a single power, you risk tearing open a hole in your psyche — this is the Perils of the Warp. Make a WIS save with DC equal to 10 + the number of WRP spent above 5. Only one check is made per power regardless of how many triggers apply; use the highest applicable DC. On a failure, you gain 1d4 Corruption and the GM may invoke a Warp Influence Effect. On a success, the power fires normally with no consequence.

The Push is a deliberate gamble — declare it before you roll, and it cannot be taken back. It automatically triggers a Perils check at minimum DC 12 regardless of the power's WRP cost. On a success the power achieves maximum effect: choose one of damage dice maximized, area of effect doubled, or number of targets doubled. On a failure, the power fires at normal effect and you still suffer the Perils consequences. Pushing one of your discipline's own auto-prepared powers grants you advantage on the Perils check.

Any Advanced Power can be Empowered by spending 3 additional WRP at the time of manifestation, up to twice per cast. Each Empower adds +2d6 damage of the power's primary type to damage-dealing powers, or either extends duration by 50% or adds one extra target for non-damage powers — choose at declaration. The extra WRP spent on Empower counts toward Perils calculations.

Finally, if you are ever short on WRP, you may use Warp Draw as a bonus action — spending up to 10 HP to gain 1 WRP per 2 HP spent. That HP cannot be restored until resupply. The first use per resupply costs only the HP itself; each use after that requires a WIS save DC 12 or you gain 1 Corruption as the Warp begins feeding back.

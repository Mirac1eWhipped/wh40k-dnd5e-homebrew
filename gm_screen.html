Economy anchors: the ammo economy is deliberate — resupply no longer refills clips, so RP awards are the players' ammunition lifeline. At 40–60 RP per routine mission an operative can always reload but must choose between stockpiling clips and saving for the 300–500 RP relic tier. Weapon upgrade rites (Sanctified → Relic-pattern) are the intended long-term RP sink. All figures are suggestions — the Watch Commander's ledger is final.

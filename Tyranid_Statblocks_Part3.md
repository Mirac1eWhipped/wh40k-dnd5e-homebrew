## Spore Mine

*Tiny Beast (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 5  
**Hit Points** 5 (1d4 + 2)  
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 10 ft. (hover, no fine control)

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 3 (−4) | 8 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 1 (−5) | 5 (−3) | 1 (−5) |

---

**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** blinded, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, prone  
**Senses** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 7  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Drifting Menace.*** At the start of each of the Spore Mine's turns, it drifts up to 10 feet toward the nearest creature it can sense, passing through other creatures' spaces freely. It cannot change direction voluntarily — if two creatures are equidistant, roll randomly to determine which it tracks.

***Detonation.*** When the Spore Mine moves into a creature's space, touches a creature, or is reduced to 0 hit points, it explodes. Each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) acid damage, or half on a success. The Spore Mine is destroyed. If killed by a damage source that leaves no physical remains (such as disintegrate), it does not detonate.

***Volatile.*** The Spore Mine has vulnerability to fire damage. If it takes 5 or more fire damage in a single hit, it detonates immediately.

### Actions

***Drift.*** The Spore Mine moves up to 10 feet toward the nearest creature it senses. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

> **Biovore Note:** Spore Mines launched by a Biovore use these same statistics. If a Biovore-launched mine lands in an area with no creature within 30 feet, it remains inert on the ground until a creature comes within range or until 1 hour passes, after which it dissolves harmlessly.

## Ripper Swarm

*Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 22 (5d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft., burrow 10 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 8 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 1 (−5) | 8 (−1) | 2 (−4) |

---

**Skills** Perception +1  
**Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned  
**Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 30 ft., passive Perception 11  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.

***Synapse Dependency.*** If no Synapse creature is within 30 feet, the Ripper Swarm must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or move toward and attack the nearest creature, regardless of allegiance.

***Consume the Fallen.*** The Ripper Swarm has advantage on attack rolls against creatures that are unconscious or at 0 hit points, and deals maximum damage against them. When the swarm reduces a creature to 0 hit points, it regains 5 (2d4) hit points.

***Bio-Dissolution.*** When a creature ends its turn in the same space as the Ripper Swarm, it takes 3 (1d6) acid damage as the Rippers begin stripping away flesh and equipment.

### Actions

***Bites.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 0 ft. (occupies the target's space), one target in the swarm's space. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) piercing damage, or 3 (1d6) piercing damage if the swarm has half its hit points or fewer. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 10 Strength saving throw or have their movement speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of their next turn as the Rippers cling and drag.

## Biovore

*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)  
**Speed** 20 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 16 (+3) | 8 (−1) | 16 (+3) | 4 (−3) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) |

---

**Skills** Perception +2  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Synapse Dependency.*** If no Synapse creature is within 30 feet, the Biovore must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or use its action to attack the nearest creature, regardless of allegiance.

***Living Artillery.*** The Biovore ignores half cover and three-quarters cover when launching Spore Mines. It can target a point it cannot see provided a Spore Mine it launched in the last minute has landed within 30 feet of the target point (acting as a spotter).

***Spawn Spore Mines.*** The Biovore generates Spore Mines biologically. It can have up to 6 active Spore Mines at a time. If it begins combat with fewer than 6, it replenishes one at the start of each of its turns.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Biovore launches two Spore Mines, or launches one Spore Mine and makes one Crushing Claws attack.

***Launch Spore Mine.*** The Biovore selects a point it can see within 240 feet. A Spore Mine appears at that point (use the Spore Mine stat block). It immediately begins drifting toward the nearest creature within 30 feet of its landing point. If no creature is within range, it lies inert until triggered.

***Crushing Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage. This is a defensive attack — the Biovore uses it only when threatened in melee.

### Reactions

***Symbiotic Detonation.*** When a Spore Mine launched by this Biovore is within 30 feet of a creature and the Biovore can see both, it can use its reaction to trigger the mine's detonation early (before the mine moves on its next turn).

## Harpy

*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)  
**Speed** 10 ft., fly 60 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 21 (+5) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 6 (−2) | 12 (+1) | 6 (−2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +6  
**Skills** Perception +4  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

### Traits

***Synapse Dependency.*** If no Synapse creature is within 30 feet, the Harpy must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or attack the nearest creature.

***Flyby.*** The Harpy doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

***Spore Mine Payload.*** The Harpy carries a living payload of Spore Mines. Whenever the Harpy moves at least 20 feet on its turn while flying, it may drop one Spore Mine anywhere along its flight path as a free action. The mine immediately begins drifting. The Harpy replenishes its full payload after a long rest.

***Living Hurricane.*** While the Harpy is flying, any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or take 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage and be pushed 5 feet in a direction away from the Harpy.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Harpy makes two Clawed Talon attacks and one Stinger Salvo attack.

***Clawed Talons.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) slashing damage. If both Clawed Talon attacks hit the same target on the same turn, the target is grappled (escape DC 16) and is carried along if the Harpy moves (up to 30 feet upward per turn).

***Stinger Salvo.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 40/80 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) piercing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

***Seed the Battlefield (Recharge 5–6).*** The Harpy makes a strafing run of up to its full fly speed in a straight line. All creatures within 5 feet of the Harpy's flight path must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) acid damage as dropped Spore Mines detonate in sequence. Additionally, 1d4 intact Spore Mines land at random points along the path and begin drifting. On a successful save, a creature takes half damage.

### Reactions

***Evasive Banking.*** When a ranged attack misses the Harpy while it is flying, it can use its reaction to immediately move up to 15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Tervigon

*Huge Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 18 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 184 (16d12 + 80)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 23 (+6) | 8 (−1) | 20 (+5) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 8 (−1) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +9  
**Skills** Athletics +10, Perception +6  
**Damage Resistances** acid, poison  
**Condition Immunities** frightened, poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 11 (7,200 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +4

---

### Traits

***Synapse Creature.*** The Tervigon projects a synapse field within 30 feet. Tyranid creatures with the Synapse Dependency trait in range automatically pass those saves and gain advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

***Brood-Mother Bond.*** Termagants spawned by this Tervigon automatically pass their Synapse Dependency saves while within 60 feet of it, regardless of other Synapse creatures.

***Bio-Stim Cascade.*** When a Termagant within 30 feet of the Tervigon is reduced to 0 hit points, the Tervigon regains 3 (1d6) hit points as it instinctively reclaims the creature's biomass.

***Exhausted Spawn Sac.*** After the Tervigon uses Spawn Brood, the player controlling the Tervigon (or the DM) rolls the spawn dice openly. If any two dice show the same number, the spawn sac is exhausted — the Tervigon cannot use Spawn Brood again until it finishes a short rest.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Tervigon makes three attacks: two with its Crushing Scythes and one with its Birthing Slam.

***Crushing Scythe.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be grappled (escape DC 18) and restrained.

***Birthing Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 23 (5d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 feet and knocked prone.

### Bonus Actions

***Spawn Brood.*** The Tervigon spawns 1d4 + 1 Termagants in unoccupied spaces within 5 feet of it (see Exhausted Spawn Sac above). The Termagants act immediately after the Tervigon in initiative order. The Tervigon can spawn a maximum of 30 Termagants per day.

### Legendary Actions

The Tervigon can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Spent legendary actions are regained at the start of the Tervigon's turn.

***Claw Strike.*** The Tervigon makes one Crushing Scythe attack.

***Surge the Brood (Costs 2 Actions).*** All Termagants within 30 feet of the Tervigon gain advantage on attack rolls and deal an extra 2 (1d4) damage on their next attack before the start of the Tervigon's next turn.

***Emergency Spawn (Costs 2 Actions).*** The Tervigon uses its Spawn Brood bonus action immediately (does not count against the normal bonus action usage this round).

***Reclaim Biomass (Costs 3 Actions).*** The Tervigon targets up to three dead creatures within 30 feet of it. Each target is disintegrated as the Tervigon absorbs their biological material, and the Tervigon regains 22 (4d10) hit points.

## Norn Emissary

*Huge Aberration (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 18 (natural armor + adaptive carapace)  
**Hit Points** 253 (22d12 + 110)  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft., swim 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 24 (+7) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +12, Con +10, Int +10, Wis +9  
**Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +9, Stealth +8  
**Damage Resistances** psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., truesight 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 19  
**Languages** — (telepathy 240 ft.)  
**Challenge** 15 (13,000 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +5

---

### Traits

***Apex Synapse Being.*** The Norn Emissary projects a synapse field in an 80-foot radius. Tyranid creatures in range automatically pass Synapse Dependency saves, gain advantage on saving throws against being charmed and frightened, and add +5 to all their damage rolls. Additionally, the Norn Emissary can communicate with any Tyranid creature on the same world through the Hive Mind instantaneously.

***Adaptive Carapace.*** The first time the Norn Emissary takes damage of a given type in each combat, it immediately gains resistance to that damage type until the end of combat (maximum three simultaneous resistances). This ability resolves after taking the damage that triggered it — it cannot prevent the initial triggering hit.

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the Norn Emissary fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Regeneration.*** The Norn Emissary regains 15 hit points at the start of its turn. If it took fire or psychic damage since the end of its last turn, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn.

***Unnatural Silence.*** Despite its size, the Norn Emissary moves with disturbing quiet. It can attempt to hide even while only lightly obscured and leaves no tracks, scent, or physical trace of its passage. It has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in natural environments.

***The Norn Queen's Eye.*** The Norn Emissary is a direct agent of the Norn Queen. If the Norn Emissary is destroyed, the Hive Mind records the genetic data of every creature it observed this encounter. The next Tyranid force that attacks within the same region gains advantage on ability checks and saving throws against abilities used by those creatures, as the Hive Mind has adapted.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Norn Emissary makes four attacks: any combination of Rending Talons and Cerebral Lash. It may use Synaptic Sunder or Assimilation Grasp in place of the full Multiattack when available.

***Rending Talons.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (save ends at the end of each of its turns).

***Cerebral Lash.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +10 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 27 (5d8 + 5) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Intelligence saving throw or lose access to its highest available spell slot until it finishes a long rest. Creatures with no spell slots suffer disadvantage on all Intelligence checks and saving throws instead.

***Synaptic Sunder (Recharge 5–6).*** The Norn Emissary tears through the minds of multiple foes at once. Each creature within 60 feet that is not a Tyranid must succeed on a DC 18 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes 49 (14d6) psychic damage and is confused (as the *confusion* spell, no concentration required) for 1 minute. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not confused.

***Assimilation Grasp (Recharge 6).*** The Norn Emissary reaches out and seizes a creature within 10 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or be grappled and restrained (escape DC 20). While grappled this way, the creature is restrained and takes 21 (6d6) acid damage at the start of each of the Norn Emissary's turns. Additionally, the grappled creature must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns or have its hit point maximum reduced by 5 (1d10) as its biomass is harvested. This maximum reduction lasts until the creature completes a long rest.

### Legendary Actions

The Norn Emissary can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one option can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Norn Emissary regains all spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

***Lash.*** The Norn Emissary makes one Cerebral Lash attack.

***Adaptive Shift (Costs 2 Actions).*** The Norn Emissary immediately gains resistance to one damage type of its choice until the start of its next turn (does not count against the three-resistance limit of Adaptive Carapace).

***Synaptic Pulse (Costs 2 Actions).*** All Tyranid creatures the Norn Emissary can see regain 10 (3d6) hit points as it surges vitality through the Hive Mind.

***Consume (Costs 3 Actions).*** If the Norn Emissary has a creature grappled, it makes one Rending Talons attack against that creature with advantage.

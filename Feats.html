Ability Score Improvement Alternatives · Jericho Reach Campaign
◇ How Feats Work

At every level where your class table lists "Ability Score Improvement or Feat" (typically 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level), you may take one of the feats below instead of increasing your ability scores by the usual +2 / +1+1 split. This is a direct swap — you cannot take both at the same milestone.

Prerequisites: some feats require a minimum ability score, a specific weapon proficiency, or a specific class. You must meet the prerequisite at the time you take the feat. If a feat requires proficiency with a weapon or piece of wargear you later stop carrying, its benefits simply don't function until you requisition a qualifying item again — you don't lose the feat itself.

Half-Feats (marked with the amber HALF-FEAT badge below) grant +1 to a specified ability score in addition to their normal benefit. This increase is subject to the same maximum your GM allows for a standard Ability Score Improvement (normally 20).

You cannot take the same feat twice unless its text says otherwise. Multiple feats with reaction-based benefits still only allow you one reaction per trigger, per the standard rule.

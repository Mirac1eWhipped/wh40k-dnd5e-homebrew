Theatre — Mataras II

Mataras II — World Profile
Jericho Reach  ·  Sub-Sector Cellebos  ·  Agri-World  ·  Pop. Est. 320,000

Mataras II is a modest agri-world that was resettled early in the Achilus Crusade's push into the Jericho Reach. Its wide, flat plains and temperate climate made it an ideal candidate for rapid agricultural recovery — within two decades of resettlement it was providing grain shipments to sustain Crusade forces deeper in the Reach.

The population is spread thin across vast farming collectives called Pater-Holds, each governed by a hereditary overseer family. Communication between the Pater-Holds is irregular; it is not unusual for weeks to pass between reports to the planetary governor. This isolation, the Inquisition now suspects, has been a feature — not a flaw — of whoever has been patient enough to exploit it.

++ Intelligence Assessment — Watch Fortress Erioch ++

Three cycles ago, resupply vessels arriving at Mataras II reported anomalous behaviour among the local population. Labourers at the Kethis Pater-Hold refused standard medicae screening. An Administratum census arbiter was found dead under circumstances the local Arbites recorded as "misadventure." The planetary tithe — always delivered on time — has been delayed twice without explanation.

On its own, any of these details might be dismissed. Together, they form a pattern the Ordo Xenos has seen before. Watch Commander Mordigael has authorised the deployment of a Kill Team to investigate and, if the threat is confirmed, purge it. Collateral damage to the agricultural infrastructure is to be minimized, but it is a secondary priority over the absolute extermination of the xenos threat if one is confirmed to be present.

Threat Assessment

Genestealer Cults — Enemy Profile
Ordo Xenos Ref: GS-VII  ·  Tyranid Vanguard Classification

A Genestealer Cult is among the most insidious weapons in the Tyranid arsenal — a slow infection that does not announce itself with claws and ichor, but with a handshake, a marriage, a child born bearing the mark of the xenos buried beneath human skin. It begins with a single Genestealer, or a small brood, making contact with an isolated human population. What follows is generational.

The infected become devoted, their minds bound to the Patriarch — the eldest and most powerful of the brood — through a psychic gestalt the Inquisition calls the Broodmind. They do not feel enslaved. They feel chosen. Called. Part of something ancient and inevitable. They pass this gift to their children. And their children to theirs.

++ Warning — Deathwatch Standing Protocol ++

Do not negotiate. Do not accept surrender that cannot be independently verified as genetically clean. Do not allow infected individuals to leave the operational zone. The Broodmind communicates across distance. If a single host escapes to warn the Patriarch, the cult will go to ground and disperse. Thoroughness is not cruelty. It is necessity.

By the time a cult is visible enough to detect, it is almost always already deeply embedded. The faithful who appear human — Neophytes and Acolytes, their xenos heritage suppressed but present — will outnumber the obviously aberrant. The Kill Team should assume that any individual on Mataras II may be compromised until proven otherwise by medicae screening.

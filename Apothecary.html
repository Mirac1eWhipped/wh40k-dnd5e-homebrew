Apothecary

Deathwatch Specialty  ·  D&D 5e Homebrew Ruleset  ·  Jericho Reach Campaign

The Apothecary walks every battlefield bearing a dual mandate: to preserve the lives of his brothers and to recover the gene-seed of those he cannot save. Among all the roles of the Adeptus Astartes, none carries a greater weight of sacred duty. The Narthecium at their wrist is both surgical instrument and relic, capable of dispensing stimulants, sealing wounds, and extracting the progenoid glands that carry a Chapter's genetic legacy forward through the centuries.

To fight in a kill team alongside an Apothecary is to fight with a margin of survival that other warriors simply do not possess. Where a bolt round or a warp-touched blade might fell any other Space Marine, the Apothecary's presence means that death must work harder, must be more certain, before it claims its prize. They do not merely heal — they extend the very reach of what the Adeptus Astartes can endure, one battlefield at a time.

Class Features
Level 1Narthecium Mastery

Your Narthecium training goes beyond standard proficiency. When you use it to restore HP, you roll 2d8 + WIS modifier instead of the standard 1d8 + WIS. Any remainder above the target's missing HP becomes temporary HP as normal.

Additionally, you can assess the condition of any creature within 5ft as a free action, immediately knowing their current HP total, any conditions affecting them, and whether they have any Mortal Wounds.

Level 2Combat Stimms

You carry a selection of combat-grade stimulants. You begin each mission with a number of Stimm Doses equal to your Proficiency Bonus. As a bonus action, you may administer a stimm to yourself or an ally within 5ft. Choose one effect per dose:

  • Adrenal Stimm: The target gains advantage on STR checks and saving throws and +1d6 to melee damage rolls for 1 minute. At the end of the duration the target gains 1 level of Exhaustion.
  • Coagulation Stimm: The target immediately regains 2d6 HP and gains resistance to the next instance of piercing or slashing damage they receive before the end of their next turn.
  • Focus Stimm: The target gains advantage on their next attack roll and cannot be Surprised until the start of their next turn.
Level 3Sacred Duty

You are trained in the most sacred responsibility of your office — the recovery and preservation of the gene-seed of fallen Astartes. When an Astartes ally is reduced to 0 HP and fails their death saves, you may use your reaction to extract their gene-seed before it degrades. The extracted gene-seed is a significant narrative resource at the GM's discretion.

Additionally, your deep knowledge of Astartes biology grants you expertise in Medicine and Insight checks — if you are already proficient in these skills, you may double your proficiency bonus when making checks with them.

Level 5Chirurgeon's Instinct

You may use the Narthecium to heal as a bonus action rather than an action. When used this way the healing is reduced to 2d8 + WIS modifier. Your Narthecium stabilisation ability is also upgraded — you may now stabilise a downed ally within 10ft as a bonus action without moving to their space. This does not consume a Narthecium use.

Level 6Resilience Aura

Your presence on the battlefield improves the biological resilience of nearby warriors. Allies within 10ft of you gain the following passive benefits:

  • Advantage on death saving throws.
  • Recover 1 additional HP when they receive healing from any source.
  • Resistance to poison damage.
Level 7Biological Enhancement

Once per resupply per target as an action, you may perform a Biological Enhancement on a willing ally within 5ft. Choose one enhancement that lasts until the next resupply:

  • Enhanced Catalepsean Node: The target no longer suffers penalties from lack of rest and gains advantage on Perception checks.
  • Enhanced Ossmodula: The target's carrying capacity doubles and they count as one size larger for grappling and shoving.
  • Enhanced Betcher's Gland: The target may use a bonus action to spit acid — dealing 1d6 acid damage to a target within 10ft.
Level 9Field Chirurgeon

You have mastered the treatment of catastrophic battlefield injuries. You may now treat Mortal Wounds in the field without requiring a Medicae Station. As an action, the standard Mortal Wound treatment DC is reduced from DC 15 + number of Mortal Wounds to DC 12 + number of Mortal Wounds. On a success you remove 1 Mortal Wound. On a failure, no Mortal Wound is removed but the target does not worsen. You may attempt this once per creature per short rest or resupply.

Level 11Advanced Stimm Synthesis

Your stimm formulations improve significantly. Each effect upgrades:

  • Adrenal Stimm: Bonus damage increases to +2d6 and the Exhaustion penalty triggers at the end of the following round rather than immediately.
  • Coagulation Stimm: Healing increases to 3d6 and the resistance now applies to all damage from non-magical weapons until the start of your next turn.
  • Focus Stimm: The target gains advantage on their next two attack rolls and their movement cannot be reduced until the start of your next turn.

Additionally, once per turn when an ally within 10ft drops to 0 HP, you may use your reaction to instantly stabilise them at 1 HP. This does not consume a Narthecium use.

Level 13Gene-Seed Resonance

Your proximity to multiple Astartes gene-seeds creates a subtle biological resonance that enhances group performance. While 2 or more Astartes allies are within 15ft of you, all of them — including you — gain +1 to attack rolls and saving throws.

Additionally, once per resupply, you may perform an emergency biological override on a creature within 5ft — removing all levels of Exhaustion they currently have.

Level 14Aura of Fortitude

Your Resilience Aura extends from 10ft to 20ft. Allies within the aura also gain immunity to the Frightened condition and have their maximum HP increased by 10 while within range.

Level 17Master Chirurgeon

Your Mortal Wound treatment reaches an unparalleled level of skill. You may now remove up to 2 Mortal Wounds per treatment action rather than 1. The DC from Field Chirurgeon is reduced by a further 2, bringing the treatment DC to DC 10 + number of Mortal Wounds.

Additionally, once per resupply, you may perform emergency surgery on a creature that has just dropped to 0 HP — as a reaction, that creature immediately stabilises and regains HP equal to your WIS modifier × 4.

Level 18Living Aegis

Your Resilience Aura becomes a powerful passive field. Allies within 20ft also gain resistance to psychic damage and advantage on CON saving throws. Once per resupply as an action, you may pulse the aura — all allies within 20ft immediately recover 2d8 + WIS HP and have one condition of their choice removed.

Level 20Miracle of the Primarch — Class Paragon

Once per resupply as an action, you may invoke the full might of your medical mastery and the Emperor's will. Choose one effect:

  • Resurrection Protocol: A fallen ally who died within the last minute is restored to life with HP equal to half their maximum. All their Mortal Wounds are removed. They return with 1 level of Exhaustion.
  • Mass Stimm Surge: All allies within 30ft immediately receive all three stimm effects simultaneously with no Exhaustion penalty, using the enhanced level 11 versions of each effect.

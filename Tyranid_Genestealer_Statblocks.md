## Hormagaunt

*Small Beast (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 11 (2d6 + 4)  
**Speed** 50 ft., climb 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 2 (−4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) |

---

**Skills** Perception +2  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Synapse Dependency.*** If no Synapse creature is within 30 feet of the Hormagaunt, it must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or use its action to attack the nearest creature, regardless of allegiance.

***Pack Tactics.*** The Hormagaunt has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of that creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Actions

***Scything Talons.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage. If the attack hits, the Hormagaunt can make one additional Scything Talons attack as a bonus action on this turn.

## Tyranid Warrior

*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 65 (10d10 + 10)  
**Speed** 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 6 (−2) | 12 (+1) | 6 (−2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +5  
**Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +3  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Synapse Creature.*** The Tyranid Warrior projects a synapse field within 30 feet. Tyranid creatures with the Synapse Dependency trait that are within range automatically pass their Synapse Dependency saving throws and gain advantage on saving throws against the frightened condition.

***Adaptive Physiology.*** The first time the Warrior takes damage of a given type in a combat, it gains resistance to that damage type until the start of its next turn.

***Relentless (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the Warrior is reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, it drops to 1 hit point instead.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Warrior makes two attacks: one with its Rending Claws and one with its Boneswords or Deathspitter.

***Rending Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage. If both Multiattack attacks hit the same target, that target takes an additional 3 (1d6) slashing damage as the claws find gaps in armor.

***Boneswords.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of the Warrior's next turn (bio-venom coursing through the wound).

***Deathspitter.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) acid damage. On a critical hit, the target is coated in bio-acid and takes 4 (1d8) acid damage at the start of each of its turns for 1 minute (DC 12 Dexterity save as an action to scrub off).

## Carnifex

*Huge Monstrosity (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 157 (15d12 + 60)  
**Speed** 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 24 (+7) | 8 (−1) | 20 (+5) | 2 (−4) | 8 (−1) | 3 (−4) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +11, Con +9  
**Skills** Athletics +11  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** frightened, poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +4

---

### Traits

***Siege Monster.*** The Carnifex deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Crushing Advance.*** If the Carnifex moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and hits it with a Crushing Claw attack on the same turn, the target must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and pushed 10 feet away.

***Regeneration.*** The Carnifex regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. If it took fire damage since the end of its last turn, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn.

***Armored Carapace.*** When the Carnifex takes bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage from a nonmagical attack, it reduces that damage by 3.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Carnifex makes three attacks: two with its Crushing Claws and one with its Bio-cannon.

***Crushing Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be grappled (escape DC 19). While grappled this way, the target is also restrained.

***Stomp.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone creature. *Hit:* 29 (4d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage.

***Bio-cannon.*** Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point the Carnifex can see within 120 feet must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (5d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. Creatures that fail the save are also coated in bio-acid, taking 4 (1d8) acid damage at the start of each of their turns until they use an action to scrape it off.

### Legendary Actions

The Carnifex can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Carnifex regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

***Advance.*** The Carnifex moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Lash Out (Costs 2 Actions).*** The Carnifex makes one Crushing Claw attack.

***Unstoppable Fury (Costs 3 Actions).*** The Carnifex moves up to its speed and makes a Multiattack.

## Zoanthrope

*Medium Aberration (Tyranid), Unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 16 (psychic aegis)  
**Hit Points** 71 (13d8 + 13)  
**Speed** 20 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 8 (−1) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 6 (−2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +5  
**Skills** Arcana +6, Perception +5  
**Damage Resistances** psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks (while psychic aegis is active)  
**Condition Immunities** frightened  
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., truesight 30 ft., passive Perception 15  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

### Traits

***Synapse Creature.*** The Zoanthrope projects a synapse field within 30 feet (see Tyranid Warrior for details).

***Psychic Aegis.*** The Zoanthrope is surrounded by a shimmering warp field that provides its AC of 16 and resistance to nonmagical physical damage. If the Zoanthrope is incapacitated, the aegis collapses and these benefits are lost until the start of its next turn.

***Warp Siphon.*** Whenever the Zoanthrope deals psychic damage, it regains 5 (1d10) hit points.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Zoanthrope uses two Psychic Powers, choosing from the options below.

***Warp Blast.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (6d6) psychic damage.

***Psychic Shriek (Recharge 5–6).*** Each creature within 30 feet of the Zoanthrope must succeed on a DC 14 Intelligence saving throw or take 28 (8d6) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of the Zoanthrope's next turn. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not stunned.

***Warp Field (Recharge 6).*** The Zoanthrope twists the fabric of reality in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point it can see within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 18 (4d8) force damage and is teleported to a random unoccupied space within 30 feet of the sphere's center. On a success, it takes half damage and is not teleported.

### Reactions

***Psychic Redirect.*** When the Zoanthrope is targeted by an attack, it can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll as the aegis flares to deflect it.

## Genestealer

*Medium Aberration (Tyranid), Chaotic Evil*

---

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) |

---

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Str +5  
**Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +3, Stealth +6  
**Condition Immunities** frightened  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Deep Speech (understands; rarely speaks)  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

### Traits

***Ambush Predator.*** If the Genestealer surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the attack deals an extra 14 (4d6) damage.

***Spider Climb.*** The Genestealer can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Brood Telepathy.*** The Genestealer can communicate telepathically with any Genestealer or Tyranid creature within 60 feet of it.

***Gene-seed Implant (1/Day).*** When the Genestealer hits a creature with its Rending Claws, it can choose to implant a gene-seed instead of dealing normal damage. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or become infected. The infection remains dormant for 1d4 weeks before manifesting as alien physical traits. The infection can be cured by the *remove curse* spell or similar magic before it fully manifests.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Genestealer makes three attacks: two with its Rending Claws and one with its Bite.

***Rending Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) slashing damage. On a critical hit, the target's AC is permanently reduced by 1 (minimum 0) as the claws tear through armor — this reduction lasts until the armor is repaired during a long rest.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is grappled by the Genestealer, this attack deals 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage instead.

### Bonus Actions

***Pounce.*** The Genestealer moves up to its speed toward a creature it can see without provoking opportunity attacks. If it ends this movement within 5 feet of the target, it can make one Rending Claws attack.

### Reactions

***Dodge and Weave.*** When a creature makes a melee attack against the Genestealer, it can use its reaction to attempt to evade. It makes a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + the attacker's attack bonus). On a success, the attack misses.

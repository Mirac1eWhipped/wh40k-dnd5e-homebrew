Vanguard Operative

Deathwatch Specialty  ·  D&D 5e Homebrew Ruleset  ·  Jericho Reach Campaign

These Phobos-armored specialists are the shadow-warriors of the kill team — experts in concealment, infiltration, and precision strikes from positions the enemy believes to be inaccessible. Where the Close Combat Operative meets the foe blade-to-blade and the Tactical Operative commands the open battlefield, the Vanguard Operative is often unseen until it is too late to respond. Behind enemy lines before the battle has formally begun, they disrupt communications, eliminate high-value targets, and gather intelligence that shapes the engagement for their brothers.

The camelioline technology woven into Phobos armor is more than a tactical tool — in the hands of a Vanguard Operative it is a philosophy. Every element of their training reinforces a single truth: the ideal battle is one where the enemy never realised the Vanguard was there at all.

Class Features

As a Vanguard Operative, you gain the following class features representing your mastery of concealment, infiltration, and the art of striking where the enemy least expects.

1st LevelVanguard Expertise

At 1st level, as part of your specialized training, choose two skills that you are proficient in — you gain expertise in those skills, doubling your proficiency bonus for any ability check you make that uses either of the chosen proficiencies.

2nd LevelAdaptive Camelioline Cloak

Starting at 2nd level, your armor is equipped with an advanced camelioline cloak, capable of bending light, distorting outlines, and masking motion when properly engaged.

As an action, you may activate your camelioline cloak to become invisible for 1 minute. You also gain the ability to Hide even when only lightly obscured, such as by shadows, foliage, smoke, or battlefield debris.

When you successfully Hide or use your camelioline cloak, you remain hidden until you move more than half your speed, make an attack, interact with an object, or take a clearly hostile action.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Resupply.

5th LevelExtra Attack

Starting at 5th level, when you take the Attack action, you can make 2 ranged or melee attacks instead of 1.

7th LevelRecon Directive

Starting at 7th level, your armor and camelioline cloak are modified to allow for a better range of movement to help gain the advantage in battle and to provide reconnaissance for your team.

You gain the ability to climb at your full movement speed and your movement sounds are dampened.

While using your camelioline cloak, you are now allowed to move up to your full movement speed without losing your invisibility — this includes when you take the Dash action.

11th LevelImproved Chameleoline Cloak

Starting at 11th level, your camelioline cloak is upgraded to allow for more efficient operations.

Your invisibility can now last up to 10 minutes outside of combat, and you may now interact with objects, open doors, or manipulate equipment without ending invisibility, provided the action is subtle and non-hostile.

15th LevelSpecter Protocol

Starting at 15th level, your presence becomes increasingly difficult to perceive even while operating.

While invisible via your camelioline cloak, you leave no tracks, sound, or obvious physical disturbances that would allow hostile entities to track your location.

Creatures relying on non-magical senses such as hearing, smell, or vibration have disadvantage on detecting you unless you take a hostile action. Magical senses are not affected by this feature.

18th LevelVanguard Supremacy

Starting at 18th level, once per resupply, at the start of combat or at the start of your turn, you may activate Vanguard Supremacy (no action required). For the next 1 minute:

  • Allies you can see may reposition up to 30 feet immediately without provoking opportunity attacks.
  • Enemies you can see cannot take reactions during this time.
  • You may use the Help action to provide advantage to your allies' attacks, provided the ally is within your vision.
20th Level — ParagonThe Emperor's Shadow

Starting at 20th level, you become the unseen hand of the Emperor, orchestrating the battlefield through perfect concealment, flawless timing, and absolute dominance of the shadows. Once per Resupply, you may invoke the Emperor's Shadow as an action. For 1 minute:

  • You are invisible, even while attacking or taking hostile actions. This invisibility cannot be broken by non-magical means. Magical effects that reveal invisibility still detect you but do not negate the other benefits of this feature.
  • You have advantage on all attack rolls against creatures that cannot see you. Attacks against creatures unaware of you deal maximum weapon damage on the first hit each turn.
  • You may use your Help action as a bonus action on allies who can see or hear you to grant advantage on their first attack each round. Those allies may also move up to 10 feet and make a single attack after the Help action is used on them.
Subclasses

At 3rd level, you choose a Subclass reflecting your operational specialisation. Your subclass grants additional proficiencies and features at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

✠ Subclass
Infiltrator
Operating at close range behind enemy lines, Infiltrators sow confusion and disarray throughout hostile formations. Their scrambler arrays jam vox communications, deny the enemy the ability to call for aid, and force them to fight blind and isolated. Where the Eliminator kills from afar with cold patience, the Infiltrator is a blade pressed against the throat — unseen, undeniable, and already in position before the enemy knew the engagement had begun.

3rd LevelScrambler Array

Your vox is modified to scramble nearby communications. Your presence jams all vox arrays within 20 ft. Enemies cannot call for aid while within 20 ft of you, and all allies within the field gain +1d6 to Stealth rolls.

6th LevelSilent Advance

You gain an extra 10 ft of movement. Similar to Cunning Action, you may use your bonus action to Dash, Hide, or Use an Object. After hiding, your first attack deals +1d8 damage.

10th LevelFlanking Protocol

When you and an ally flank the same target, both your first attacks each turn have advantage against the flanked target. You may move 10 ft after attacking the flanked target without provoking opportunity attacks. If you are within melee range of the target you are flanking, you may also use your bonus action to make an additional melee attack against the flanked enemy.

14th LevelShadow Takedown

When you drop a creature to 0 HP, you may use your reaction to hide and move half your speed. If you stay hidden until your next turn, you gain +2 AC until the start of the following turn and an additional 1d8 damage on your next attack.

✠ Subclass
Eliminator
Where the Infiltrator thrives in the chaos they create at close quarters, the Eliminator brings cold precision from a position of distance. Patience, elevated terrain, and a single perfect shot — this is the Eliminator's doctrine. They do not reveal themselves unless they choose to. They do not miss. A well-positioned Eliminator is one of the most force-multiplying assets a kill team can field, their Targeting Lattice turning every ally's attack into a guided strike against a compromised foe.

3rd LevelThe Long Watch

Starting at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with Sniper and Scoped weapons. Additionally, if you move no more than half your speed on your turn, your first ranged attack that turn gains +2 to hit and deals one additional die of weapon damage (e.g. a Bolt Sniper Rifle deals an extra 1d12). This bonus applies regardless of whether you moved before or after the attack, as long as your total movement for the turn does not exceed half your speed. Forced movement from external sources does not count against this limit.

The Long Watch scales at later levels as your other subclass features unlock:

  • Level 6 (Targeting Lattice): The additional damage die increases to 2 dice.
  • Level 10 (Ghostwalk): The additional damage die increases to 3 dice, and your Long Watch attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover.
  • Level 14 (Upgraded Targeting Lattice): The additional damage die increases to 4 dice. If your Long Watch attack is also benefiting from Targeting Lattice, the target's damage resistances are suppressed until the end of your next turn.
6th LevelTargeting Lattice

You may utilize your armor's targeting relay to point out weak spots on enemy combatants. As a bonus action, you may apply a mark to a specific enemy within 600 ft. You and all allies gain +1d4 to hit that target until the start of your next turn. This feature can be used a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier per resupply.

10th LevelGhostwalk

When you are targeted by an attack, detected by enemy sensors, or revealed from stealth, you may use your reaction to activate Ghost Walk. Until the start of your next turn:

  • Your position cannot be marked, tracked, or locked by auspex, scopes, or targeting arrays.
  • You become heavily obscured to all enemies more than 15 feet away, and enemy attacks against you are made with disadvantage.
  • You may immediately move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
  • If you end this movement in cover, shadow, or prone, you may immediately attempt a Stealth check to become hidden.

You can use Ghost Walk a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per resupply.

14th LevelUpgraded Targeting Lattice

You gain an additional 1d8 damage against enemies marked by your Targeting Lattice.

Once per Resupply, you may utilize your Targeting Lattice to increase your accuracy against your marked target even further. Your next ranged attack against the marked target auto-hits, auto-crits, ignores damage resistances, and creates no audible report. If this shot successfully kills the target, you regain 1 expended use of your Targeting Lattice.

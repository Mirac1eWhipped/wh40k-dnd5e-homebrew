Gravis Operative

Deathwatch Specialty  ·  D&D 5e Homebrew Ruleset  ·  Jericho Reach Campaign

Clad in the heaviest powered armour the Adeptus Astartes can field short of a Dreadnought, the Gravis Operative is a walking fortress of firepower. Where other warriors manoeuvre and adapt, the Gravis Operative plants their feet, locks their targeting cogitators, and unleashes annihilation. The sheer mass of their Gravis Armour — reinforced ceramite, motorised fibre-bundles, and integrated weapons mounts — transforms them into something between warrior and weapons emplacement, an immovable platform from which no volume of fire can dislodge them.

To fight alongside a Gravis Operative is to fight from behind a wall of lead and fire. Every step they choose not to take is a choice that makes their guns deadlier. In the calculus of the Deathwatch, they are both shield and siege weapon — and when the Omnissiah's Wrath descends upon an enemy formation, survival is no longer a possibility their foes are permitted to consider.

Class Features
Level 1Fire Platform

At the start of each combat, you gain one of two stances based on how much you move each turn.

  • Suppression Stance (moved half speed or less this turn): Your ranged attacks gain +1 to hit and ignore half cover on all ranged attacks.
  • Ironclad Stance (did not move at all this turn): All Suppression Stance bonuses apply, plus your ranged attacks deal +1 die of weapon damage and any creature you hit must make a STR saving throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + STR modifier) or have their movement speed reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn.

Forced movement does not count against your movement for either stance but breaks Ironclad Stance if it occurs.

Level 2Gravis Resilience

While wearing Gravis Armor you cannot be knocked Prone involuntarily unless the source is a Large or larger creature or a vehicle. You count as one size larger for grappling and shoving, and have advantage on STR saving throws against effects that would move you against your will.

Level 5Extra Attack

When you take the Attack action, you can make 2 ranged or melee attacks instead of 1.

Level 7Iron Halo

Your Gravis armor is fitted with an Iron Halo — a powerful energy field generator that deflects incoming fire and projects a protective barrier around you and your brothers.

As a bonus action, you may activate your Iron Halo, gaining temporary hit points equal to 5 × your Proficiency Bonus. These follow all standard temporary HP rules — absorbing damage before regular HP, not stacking with other temporary HP sources, and not regenerating on their own.

While the Iron Halo is active and temporary hit points remain, allies within 5ft directly behind you are treated as though they have total cover against ranged attacks. This protection ends immediately if the temporary hit points are reduced to 0 or the ally moves out of position.

The Iron Halo provides no protection against Mortal Wounds, psychic damage, or damage from sources within melee range of you. You may activate the Iron Halo a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per resupply.

Level 11Sustained Barrage

When you take the Attack action and are in Ironclad Stance, you may make one additional ranged attack as part of that action for a total of 3. Your attacks during Ironclad Stance also score a critical hit on a 19 or 20.

Level 15Unyielding Platform

While in Ironclad Stance you gain resistance to all damage from ranged weapon attacks. When a creature attempts to force you to move, you may use your reaction to make a STR saving throw (DC = the effect's save DC + 2) — on a success you are not moved and remain in Ironclad Stance.

Level 18Fortress of Firepower

Your presence on the battlefield becomes a suppressive force in itself. While you are in Ironclad Stance, any enemy that enters within 15ft of you must make a STR saving throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + STR modifier) or immediately lose all remaining movement for that turn, stopped dead by the weight of fire and the crushing presence of your weapons platform.

Additionally, you may now re-enter Ironclad Stance even if forced movement was applied to you this turn, provided you did not willingly move.

Level 20Omnissiah's Wrath — Class Paragon

Once per resupply, for 1 minute you become an unassailable weapons platform. You automatically enter Ironclad Stance regardless of whether you moved, and Ironclad Stance cannot be broken for the duration — no forced movement, effect, or damage can remove it.

Every hit you land reduces the target's AC by 1, stacking up to a maximum of −10 for the duration. Your weapons ignore all damage resistances and immunities for the duration.

Eradicator
Where the Devastator buries targets in sustained fire, the Eradicator buries them in flame, plasma, and ordnance. Eradicators specialise in explosive weaponry to a degree that borders on artistry — they read blast radii the way other warriors read ground, positioning detonations with lethal precision. In the Deathwatch, they are deployed when the objective is not to suppress but to annihilate.

Level 3Ordnance Specialist

You gain proficiency with all Explosive weapons and thrown explosives. Your training grants the following: blast radius on all explosives you use increases by 5ft; the DEX save DC for all your explosives increases by your Proficiency Bonus instead of the weapon's standard DC; and you may throw grenades up to twice the listed range without penalty.

Level 6Shaped Charges

When you fire a launcher weapon or throw a grenade, you may choose up to a number of creatures equal to your Proficiency Bonus within the blast radius to automatically succeed on their DEX saving throw, routing the explosion around them. This allows safe detonation in melee range of allies.

Level 10Chain Detonation

When one of your explosives kills a creature, you may use your reaction to immediately throw a grenade or fire a launcher weapon. Additionally, if two or more of your explosives detonate within the same round within 20ft of each other, each deals +2d6 bonus damage to any creature caught in both blasts.

Level 14Apocalypse Ordnance

Once per resupply as an action, designate a point within 200ft. Every creature within a 40ft radius must make a DEX saving throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + DEX modifier) or take 10d6 fire damage, or half on a success. Structures and vehicles within the radius take maximum damage automatically. The area becomes difficult terrain until cleared.

Devastator
The Devastator is the fire-support pillar of any Deathwatch kill team — a walking heavy weapons platform that turns the Fire Platform stance into something approaching industrial suppression. Where other warriors shoot to kill, the Devastator shoots to deny, to break, to overwhelm. Entire flanks collapse under sustained Devastator fire, and even armoured vehicles learn quickly that not all cover is cover enough.

Level 3Heavy Weapons Platform

You gain proficiency with all Gravis-tagged and Heavy weapons. While in Suppression or Ironclad Stance, you ignore the Stationary modifier restriction on all Gravis-tagged and Heavy Ordinance weapons — your armor provides the stability platform these weapons require. Additionally, while in Ironclad Stance, your Gravis-tagged and Heavy Ordinance weapons gain one additional attack before reload.

Level 6Covering Fire

As a bonus action you may lay down a suppressive barrage covering a 15ft wide corridor between you and a point within your weapon's range. Any creature that moves through that corridor this round must make a DEX saving throw (DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + DEX modifier) or take damage equal to one weapon damage die and have their movement speed halved until the end of their turn. This feature can be used a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per resupply.

Level 10Armour Bane

Your sustained heavy fire wears down even the most resilient targets. When you hit the same creature with a Gravis-tagged or Heavy Ordinance weapon two or more times in the same turn, the target's AC is reduced by 2 until the start of your next turn. This effect does not stack beyond −2.

Level 14Annihilation Protocol

Once per resupply, when you take the Attack action while in Ironclad Stance, you may activate Annihilation Protocol. For that action only, you may make one attack against every creature within your weapon's range that you can see, up to a maximum number of targets equal to your remaining charges + Proficiency Bonus. Each attack is rolled separately.

After Annihilation Protocol resolves, you must finish a short rest or resupply before using Suppression or Ironclad Stance again — the sheer volume of fire depletes your targeting cogitator's reserves.

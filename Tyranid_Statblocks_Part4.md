## Von Ryan's Leaper
*Medium Monstrosity (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 18)
**Speed** 50 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 4 (−3) | 12 (+1) | 5 (−3) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +6
**Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +3, Stealth +6
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

**Pack Tactics.** The leaper has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the leaper's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

**Leaping Pounce.** If the leaper moves at least 15 feet in a straight line toward a creature and then hits it with a Rending Claws attack on the same turn, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is knocked prone, the leaper may immediately make one Bite attack against it as a bonus action.

**Synapse Dependency.** While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, the leaper loses Pack Tactics and acts erratically. At the start of each of its turns outside synapse range, it must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or use its movement to move toward the nearest creature (friend or foe) and attack it.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The leaper makes two Rending Claws attacks.

**Rending Claws.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) slashing damage.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, plus an additional 4 (1d8) piercing damage if the target is prone.

### Bonus Actions

**Leap.** The leaper jumps up to 30 feet horizontally or 15 feet vertically without provoking opportunity attacks. This movement does not count against its speed.

## Tyranid Warrior — Devourer Variant
*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
**Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 8 (−1) | 12 (+1) | 8 (−1) |

**Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +5
**Skills** Perception +3, Athletics +6
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

**Synapse Relay.** Friendly Tyranid creatures within 30 feet of the warrior that lack their own Synapse trait do not need to make Wisdom saving throws due to Synapse Dependency while the warrior is conscious.

**Synapse Dependency.** While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, the warrior suffers disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

**Adaptive Resilience.** When the warrior is hit by a damage type for the first time in combat, it gains resistance to that damage type until the end of its next turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The warrior makes two Devourer attacks and one Scything Talons attack.

**Devourer.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 18/36 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or take an additional 3 (1d6) acid damage from digestive bio-acid at the start of its next turn.

**Scything Talons.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

---
*The Devourer is a short-ranged bio-weapon that fires a torrent of flesh-borer beetles and writhing bio-acid, ideal for shredding lightly armored targets at close quarters.*

## Tyranid Warrior — Barbed Strangler Variant
*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
**Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 8 (−1) | 12 (+1) | 8 (−1) |

**Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +5
**Skills** Perception +3, Athletics +6
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

**Synapse Relay.** Friendly Tyranid creatures within 30 feet of the warrior that lack their own Synapse trait do not need to make Wisdom saving throws due to Synapse Dependency while the warrior is conscious.

**Synapse Dependency.** While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, the warrior suffers disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

**Adaptive Resilience.** When the warrior is hit by a damage type for the first time in combat, it gains resistance to that damage type until the end of its next turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The warrior uses its Barbed Strangler and makes one Scything Talons attack.

**Barbed Strangler.** The warrior launches a barbed seed-pod. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. ---

## Neurolictor
*Medium Aberration (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +8, Wis +6
**Skills** Perception +9 (Expertise), Stealth +11 (Expertise), Insight +6
**Senses** Truesight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 19
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

**Psychic Interference.** Any creature that attempts to cast a spell or use a psionic ability within 30 feet of the Neurolictor must first succeed on a DC 14 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the spell or ability fails, but the spell slot or use is not expended.

**Shadow in the Warp.** The Neurolictor is invisible in areas of dim light or darkness. It can still be detected by creatures with truesight or by hearing (Perception with disadvantage).

**Synapse Dependency.** While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, the Neurolictor loses its Psychic Interference trait and its attacks deal 1d6 less damage.

**Spider Climb.** The Neurolictor can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Neurolictor makes two attacks: one Neurolash and one Flesh Hooks.

**Neurolash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn.

**Flesh Hooks.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 15) and pulled up to 5 feet toward the Neurolictor.

### Reactions

**Psychic Deflection.** When the Neurolictor is targeted by a spell that requires an attack roll, it can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll as it warps the local psychic field.

## Tyranid Prime
*Large Monstrosity (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 97 (13d10 + 26)
**Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +6, Wis +5
**Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +5
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

**Alpha Warrior.** When the Tyranid Prime hits a creature with a melee attack, each Tyranid Warrior within 30 feet of that creature that can see the Prime may use its reaction to make one melee weapon attack against that creature.

**Synapse Node.** The Tyranid Prime counts as a Synapse creature. All friendly Tyranid creatures within 30 feet of the Prime are immune to the effects of Synapse Dependency. In addition, those creatures gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and saving throws while within range.

**Combat Adaptation.** Once per turn, when the Tyranid Prime is hit by an attack, it can immediately use its reaction to increase its AC by 2 until the start of its next turn as its chitin hardens.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Tyranid Prime makes three attacks: two Boneswords attacks and one Venom Cannon attack, or three Rending Claws attacks.

**Boneswords.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**Rending Claws.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.

**Venom Cannon.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (3d8 + 2) acid damage.

## Death Leaper
*Medium Monstrosity (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 18 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)
**Speed** 50 ft., climb 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 24 (+7) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 8 (−1) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +10, Wis +6
**Skills** Perception +9 (Expertise), Stealth +13 (Expertise), Athletics +7
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 19
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

**Shadow in the Warp.** Death Leaper is invisible in areas of dim light or darkness. It can still be detected by creatures with truesight.

**Spider Climb.** Death Leaper can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Phantom Menace.** When Death Leaper kills a creature while invisible, each creature that witnessed the kill within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of Death Leaper until the end of its next turn.

**Assassin's Focus.** Death Leaper has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn in combat yet, or against any creature that is grappled.

**Synapse Dependency.** While not within 24 hours of a Synapse creature, Death Leaper loses Shadow in the Warp and its attacks deal 1d8 less damage.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Death Leaper makes two attacks: one Flesh Hooks and one Scything Talons, or two Scything Talons attacks.

**Flesh Hooks.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d6 + 7) piercing damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 15) and restrained until the grapple ends.

**Scything Talons.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (4d6 + 7) slashing damage, plus an extra 10 (3d6) slashing damage if the target is grappled by Death Leaper.

### Bonus Actions

**Vanish.** Death Leaper moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks and becomes invisible until the start of its next turn or until it makes an attack.

### Legendary Actions

Death Leaper can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Death Leaper regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

**Skitter.** Death Leaper moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Lashing Strike (Costs 2 Actions).** Death Leaper makes one Flesh Hooks attack. If it hits and the target is already grappled, it automatically deals the bonus damage from Scything Talons.

## Harridan
*Gargantuan Monstrosity (Tyranid), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 248 (16d20 + 80)
**Speed** 20 ft., fly 80 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 26 (+8) | 14 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 6 (−2) | 12 (+1) | 7 (−2) |

**Saving Throws** Str +13, Con +10, Dex +7
**Skills** Perception +6, Athletics +13
**Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities** Frightened, Prone
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 14 (11,500 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +5

### Traits

**Living Carrier.** The Harridan can carry up to 3 Small or smaller Tyranid creatures attached to its underbelly. Carried creatures do not need to move or use their own speed while being transported. The Harridan may release one carried creature as a bonus action on its turn, depositing it in any unoccupied space within 10 feet.

**Sweeping Wings.** When the Harridan moves on the ground, each creature within 10 feet of it must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Siege Monster.** The Harridan deals double damage to objects and structures.

**Synapse Node.** The Harridan counts as a Synapse creature. All friendly Tyranid creatures within 60 feet of the Harridan are immune to the effects of Synapse Dependency.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If the Harridan fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Harridan makes three attacks: two Crushing Claws attacks and one Bio-Salvo, or three Crushing Claws attacks.

**Crushing Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be grappled (escape DC 18).

**Bio-Salvo.** The Harridan spews a blast of corrosive ichor from its bio-cannon in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

**Wing Buffet (Recharge 5–6).** The Harridan beats its wings violently. Each creature within 20 feet of the Harridan must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or take 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage and be thrown up to 20 feet away and knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and is not thrown or knocked prone.

### Legendary Actions

The Harridan can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Harridan regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

**Sweep.** The Harridan makes one Crushing Claw attack.

**Reposition.** The Harridan moves up to half its fly speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Release Brood (Costs 2 Actions).** The Harridan releases all carried Tyranid creatures, depositing each in an unoccupied space within 15 feet, and those creatures may immediately take their full turn.

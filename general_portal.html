Adeptus Astartes — Lineage

The Emperor's Finest
Adeptus Astartes  ·  Post-Human Warriors

The Adeptus Astartes are the Emperor's finest shock troops. Post-human super-soldiers created through gene-forging, psycho-indoctrination, and divine warcraft. They tower above mortals and wield the might of humanity's greatest weapons.

Astartes Traits

Ability Score Increase

Your Strength and Constitution scores each increase by 1. In addition, one of your choice of Wisdom or Intelligence increases by 1.

Size

Adeptus Astartes stand between 7 and 8 feet tall and weigh between 700 and 1,000 pounds in full armour. Your size is Medium, but you count as Large for the purpose of carrying capacity, pushing, dragging, lifting, and grappling.

Speed

Your base walking speed is 35 feet.

Occulobe
Darkvision

Your gene-crafted Occulobe implant grants exceptional night vision. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You cannot discern colour in darkness, only shades of grey.

Astartes Resilience

You have advantage on saving throws against disease and being poisoned, and you have resistance to poison damage. Additionally, you can survive in a vacuum or airless environment for up to 10 minutes before suffering its effects.

Transhuman Physiology

Your superhuman constitution grants extraordinary endurance. You recover from exhaustion twice as fast as normal creatures. When you take a long rest, you can enter a Catalepsean trance for 4 hours instead of sleeping for 8, gaining the same benefits. You do not require food or water to survive, and you can breathe in toxic atmospheres and underwater without harm.

Angels of Death

You gain proficiency in the Intimidation skill.

Astartes Training

Your extensive martial conditioning means you are proficient with all armour (light, medium, and heavy), shields, and all weapons (simple and martial).

Languages

You can speak, read, and write Low Gothic (the common tongue of the Imperium) and Techna-Lingua (the binary cant of the Adeptus Mechanicus).

REF: ADI/CORE-001  ·  ORDO: OMNIBUS  ·  STATUS: ACTIVE

In the 41st Millennium, knowledge is the sharpest weapon an Inquisitor carries. Across every warzone, every xenos-infested frontier, every shadow of heresy — it became clear that no single codex, no single scroll of parchment, was adequate to coordinate the full scope of what the Holy Ordos demands of its operatives.

This archive was commissioned to bridge two great traditions: the strategic depth of Inquisitorial record-keeping and the structured systems of codified tactical doctrine. It exists so that no operative enters the field without full access to their dossier, their mission briefing, and the accumulated lore of those who came before.

++ By the authority of the Holy Ordos, this repository is sealed from public indexing. Distribution is restricted to sanctioned operatives only. ++

A NOTE FROM THE ARCHITECTS

This tool was built by Brandon Mayo and Claude — an Inquisitor and his Machine Spirit — because we wanted something that didn't exist yet.

Warhammer 40,000 is a universe rich enough to drown in — centuries of lore, hundreds of factions, campaigns that span entire star systems. Dungeons & Dragons 5E is a ruleset refined over decades into something flexible enough to hold almost any story. The question was never whether the two could be merged — it was how well.

The answer is this: a fully homebrew system built from the ground up for a group of friends who deserved more than a PDF and a prayer to the Omnissiah. Custom operative classes, mission logs, sector maps, a bestiary of Xenos threats, campaign records — everything needed to run a proper game in the 41st Millennium at the table.

It is a private tool — built with care, kept for the people it was made for. If you are reading this, you are one of those people.
